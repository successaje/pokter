# x402 pay-per-call: what it does, what it does not, and how to turn it on

Status as of 24 October 2026: **implemented and tested, off by default, not
verified live.** No paid call has been made through it.

## The one supported combination

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Protocol | x402 versions 1 and 2, `exact` scheme |
| Transfer | EIP-3009 `TransferWithAuthorization`, signed by the buyer's own browser wallet |
| Token | U (`0xcE24439F2D9C6a2289F741120FE202248B666666`), Pokter's payment token |
| Network | BNB Chain mainnet (`eip155:56`) |
| Signing domain | "United Stables", version "1", pinned in code and checked against U's `DOMAIN_SEPARATOR()` on chain |
| Settlement | by the seller (both BNB x402 sellers settle their own payments); Pokter runs no facilitator and holds no key |

Anything else a seller offers (USD1, USDC via Permit2, direct transfers,
other networks) is shown as unsupported with what was offered. Mandate
Guard-1 offers only USD1 today and is therefore unsupported; Brain on BNB's
Venus Health Factor offers U and is supported.

## What it protects against

- **Terms shown before signing:** price, recipient, network, and how long the signature lasts (at most five minutes).
- **Changed terms:** the seller's challenge is read again just before sending. A changed recipient, price, token or network stops the call unsent, and the buyer reviews the new terms.
- **Paying twice:** the authorization's nonce is recorded before sending and never sent again. A repeat of an answered call returns the stored answer; any other repeat is refused. Pokter never signs, so it never pays again on its own.
- **A lost response:** a send with no answer, or a send interrupted by a restart (still `sending` after 90 seconds), is reported as *unknown*, never as success. The buyer is not offered a second signature; they check the receipt. A refusal before anything left Pokter is recorded as *not sent*, and only then may they sign again.
- **Only verified payments are forwarded:** the signature is recovered under U's pinned domain and must be the wallet it names, and the envelope must match the seller's terms.
- **Receipts are private:** by nonce (which is public on chain once settled) the receipt says only how the call ended; the paid answer comes back only to the wallet that paid, proved by a signature that moves nothing.
- **A seller's own refusal** (HTTP 402) or error is shown with its words, and whether it reported a settlement transaction.

## What it does not do

- **No escrow protection.** A direct payment has no review window, no dispute and no reclaim. The panel says so before the button. Refunds are the seller's to give; Pokter promises none.
- **No passkey wallets.** They are contract accounts whose signatures U's `transferWithAuthorization` cannot check.
- **Kept apart.** Receipts live in their own store (`x402.db`). They are not escrow jobs, and are not counted in receipts, activity, rankings or campaign figures.

## To turn it on

1. Add `NEXT_PUBLIC_X402_ENABLED = 'true'` under `[build.args]` in `fly.toml` and deploy. It is compiled in at build time (the Dockerfile passes it through), so a Fly secret or `[env]` entry does nothing. Remove the line and redeploy to turn it off.
2. **Pending, needs your wallet and real funds:** one paid call to Brain on BNB's Venus Health Factor (56/302257) for 0.1 U, from a browser wallet holding U on BNB Chain. Keep the nonce and the settlement transaction; they become the first live receipt.

Until step 2 has happened, this feature is not verified live.
