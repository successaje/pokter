## Pinned post

Post once the domain resolves, then pin it.

```
Pokter is live.

BNB Chain has 309,000+ registered agents. Finding one was never the hard part
— knowing which deserves your capital is.

The top-scored agents in the registry carry zero attestations. Plenty of them
describe real strategies and have never answered a single request.

So Pokter separates what an agent claims from what has been observed about it,
and refuses to rank what it cannot verify.

We decode on-chain attestations, probe every agent on a schedule, and score on
five dimensions — two of which stay permanently marked "not measured", because
nobody publishes realised returns and we won't infer them from uptime.

Then you hire with permissions you set: an allowlist, a spend cap, an expiry,
enforced by the account contract and revocable whenever you want.

pokter.xyz
```

Then a short reply thread under it, one point per post:

1. A screenshot of the claim-versus-evidence panel — a confident agent pitch
   beside **0 of 72 probes answered**. Caption: *"This agent describes Venus
   liquidation protection in convincing technical detail. It has never
   answered us. Hiring it is blocked."*
2. The permission panel, showing the **Cannot call** list. Caption: *"Enforced
   by the Altana account contract, not by our interface. A call outside the
   allowlist reverts on chain. We could not weaken it if we wanted to."*
3. The score breakdown with performance and risk greyed out. Caption:
   *"Two of five dimensions are permanently unscored. Nobody publishes agent
   returns, so we don't invent them."*

That third post is the one that will get quoted. Leading with what you refuse
to measure is unusual enough that people stop on it.

## Notes

**Two Flare-era posts should go.** Not because they are bad posts — because
they name a different chain, and a visitor two posts deep during a BNB
campaign should not have to wonder which chain this is. Everything else stays:
generic build-in-public posts are what prove the account is not new.

**Do not buy followers or run engagement pods.** BNB's Phase 2 rules exclude
sybil and wash activity and say encouraging artificial activity can disqualify
you from every prize. It applies to social proof as much as on-chain activity,
and it is not worth the risk for a number nobody checks.

**Grab `@pokterxyz` on Telegram too.** Damian and Gwen both work there, BNB's
community lives there, and matching handles is most of what makes a project
look real rather than assembled the night before.

**Keep the tone flat.** The product's whole argument is that it does not
overstate. An account that hypes will contradict the thing it is linking to.
